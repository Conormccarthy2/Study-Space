Our Study Advice and Tips

Try one of these tips in your next study session!

Firstly, Plan Your Session!

Set one clear goal

Pick something you can finish in one session, like answering five practice questions or outlining one section of an essay.

Study in short blocks

Try 20 minutes on one task, followed by a five-minute break. Keep electronic devices out of sight.

How To Revise What You Have Learned

Test yourself from memory

Close your notes and write down the things you remember, Check them against your notes and turn anything you missed into a practice question.

Spread revision across several days

Set a reminder to revisit the topic tomorrow and again later in the week. Start each review by answering a few questions from memory.

Practise with past exam questions

Answer questions under a time limit. Check the marking scheme and add one thing to improve to your to-do list.
